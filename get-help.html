Get Help

If you or your family need help in Eastern Idaho, these organizations are ready to assist you today. Every number on this page was verified against the organization's own information.

If you are in immediate danger or crisis

These lines answer 24 hours a day, every day. If you are in immediate physical danger, call 911.

Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

988

Call or text 988, any time. Free and confidential. Spanish available by text and chat.

Domestic Violence & Sexual Assault Center — Idaho Falls

(208) 235-2412

24-hour crisis line. Office: (208) 529-4352.

Family Services Alliance — Pocatello

(208) 251-4357

24-hour crisis line. Office: (208) 232-0742.

Bingham Crisis Center — Blackfoot

(208) 681-8712

24-hour crisis line. En español: (208) 681-8715.

Family Crisis Center — Rexburg

(208) 356-0065

24-hour hotline serving Madison, Fremont, Jefferson, and Clark counties.

Child Protection — Idaho

(855) 552-5437

24-hour statewide line to report concerns about a child's safety.

Not sure where to start?

One call connects you to help across the whole state — food, rent, utilities, health care, childcare, and employment. If you only make one call, make it this one.

211 Idaho CareLine

Dial 2-1-1

Or call (800) 926-2588, or text your ZIP code to 898211.

Hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. MT. This line is not staffed overnight or on weekends — for after-hours crisis help, use the numbers above.

healthandwelfare.idaho.gov/services-programs/211

Idaho Health & Welfare — Benefits

(877) 456-1233

Apply for SNAP (food stamps), Medicaid, cash assistance, and child care help. Idaho Falls office: 150 Shoup Ave.

Some services are phone-only — call before visiting in person.

About Faith-N-Hope Neighbors

We are a small, volunteer-run organization still building our capacity. We are not an emergency service and cannot respond quickly to urgent needs.

If you need help today, please use the resources on this page — they are staffed and ready to assist you now. We share them because getting you to the right door matters more than getting you to ours.
